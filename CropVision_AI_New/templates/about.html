🌱 About CropVision AI

CropVision AI is an intelligent agricultural platform designed to help farmers understand crop productivity and make better farming decisions using machine learning.

The system analyzes important agricultural factors such as crop type, region, soil type, season, rainfall, temperature, humidity, soil nutrients, fertilizer, irrigation and sunshine hours to estimate crop yield.

By combining a trained machine-learning model with a simple and user-friendly web interface, CropVision AI makes agricultural prediction technology easier to use and understand.
